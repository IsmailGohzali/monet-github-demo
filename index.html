✦ FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to download an app?

No. You can chat with Monet on WhatsApp, forward emails, or use the web dashboard.

Is my financial data safe?

Monet only reads the receipts and notifications you connect, and never moves money on your behalf.

What if Monet gets something wrong?

When an amount, date, or account is unclear, Monet asks you before saving. You can edit any record at any time.

Is the investing advice licensed?

No. Tips are educational only. Monet suggests product types and recommends choosing OJK-registered products.
